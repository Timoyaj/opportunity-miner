# Upwork Intelligence Skill

Extracts commercial demand signals from Cloudflare-safe RSS feeds and manual JSON drops (`data/imports/upwork/`).

## Capabilities:
- Parses client budgets and hourly rates.
- Extracts required skills (Python, pandas, Excel, ETL).
- Treats job postings as evidence of market willingness-to-pay.
