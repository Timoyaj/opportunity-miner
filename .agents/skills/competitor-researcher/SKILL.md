# Competitor Researcher Skill

Investigates market alternatives for high-priority opportunities.

## Key Question:
> **Why might someone choose a tailored solution instead of what already exists?**

Identifies:
- Overpriced incumbent SaaS tools
- Tools that are too complex or bloated for small operators
- Fragile no-code workarounds that frequently break
