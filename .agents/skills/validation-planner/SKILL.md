# Validation Planner Skill

Prepares customer discovery interviews and outreach campaigns.

## Directives:
- Generate non-leading interview questions to gauge actual customer cost and willingness to pay.
- Draft consultative, non-spammy direct messages referencing the user's exact problem and offering a quick diagnostic breakdown.
